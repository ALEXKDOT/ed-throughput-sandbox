## Review policy

The reviewer treated the implementation, model, worker messages, state, exports, and
documentation as untrusted. `PASS` is used only where a command, automated test, source
inspection, or recorded browser observation supports the stated scope. `BLOCKED` means
the required external state does not exist. `PENDING` is retained where the requested
manual, longitudinal, or deployed check was not performed.

- **P0:** release blocker: materially wrong/unsafe model, import, primary journey, or
  disclosure.
- **P1:** high: major contract failure or credible incorrect/unverifiable result path.
- **P2:** medium: material resilience, usability, documentation, or hardening gap.
- **P3:** low: bounded polish or maintainability issue.
